FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist answers your incoming business calls, speaks with customers in natural conversation, and handles common tasks like answering questions, capturing information, and booking appointments.

Do I need to build or set anything up myself?

No. CallLeah is a fully managed service. You tell us about your business and what you want handled — we build, customize, test, and deploy your AI receptionist for you.

Can CallLeah answer calls after business hours?

Yes. CallLeah can answer calls 24/7, including nights, weekends, and holidays, so customers get a response even when your team isn't available.

Can it answer questions about my business?

Yes. We configure CallLeah with your business information — services, hours, pricing, and FAQs — so it can answer common customer questions accurately.

Can CallLeah book appointments?

Yes, on the Business and Pro plans. CallLeah checks your calendar availability and books appointments directly during the call.

Can it capture leads?

Yes. CallLeah collects caller name, contact details, and the reason for the call, so your team has everything it needs to follow up.

Can I see what happened during calls?

Yes. Every call is logged in your dashboard with a summary, outcome, and the information that was captured.

How does CallLeah know about my business?

During your consultation, you tell us about your business — your services, common workflows, and how you'd like calls handled. We take it from there and build your receptionist around that information, so it responds the way your business actually operates.

Which businesses can use CallLeah?

CallLeah is built for service-based businesses that rely on phone calls — including dental clinics, HVAC companies, plumbers, salons, home service providers, cleaning companies, law firms, and other clinics.
